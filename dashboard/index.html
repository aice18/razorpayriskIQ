3DS vs Non-3DS Liability Shift Architecture

Cross-border merchants face asymmetric financial risks depending on card authorization mode:

3DS Authenticated (Issuer Liability Shift):
When OTP / 3DS challenge succeeds, 100% of unauthorized fraud chargeback liability shifts from the merchant to the foreign issuing bank (e.g. Chase, Barclays, Emirates NBD).
Non-3DS Frictionless (100% Merchant Liability):
If a fraud chargeback occurs on frictionless non-3DS, the merchant loses 100% of the funds plus a network penalty fine. Razorpay RiskIQ dynamically challenges foreign non-3DS traffic with high ML risk (>0.40) to enforce liability shift.
Visa RDR & Pre-Dispute Deflection

Razorpay SHIELD covers fraud chargebacks, but NOT service chargebacks (e.g., friendly fraud, delayed international logistics, recurring subscription disputes).

Visa Rapid Dispute Resolution (RDR):
Integrates directly with Visa and Ethoca pre-dispute networks to auto-resolve disputes within 2 hours before they are filed as official chargebacks.
Excessive Chargeback Monitoring Program (ECMP):
Keeps the merchant's chargeback ratio at 0.24% (well below Visa/Mastercard's dangerous 0.90% threshold), preventing merchant account freezing or fine tiers.
